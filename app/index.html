Roadmap to the platinum — 7 stages, plus the missable verdict (click to open)

Play in this order. Story first, then strangers, then the open-world grind — the platinum pops with 100% completion.

  1. Stage 1 — Story. Chapters 1–4 and the final mission (14 trophies). Play the story straight through; do the watch-list beats when they appear.
  2. Stage 2 — Stranger strands. 8 strands across Leonida (8 trophies). Finish them before the finale — the game may change the open world after the credits.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Stunts, shooting range, drone races, races, arcade, bowling, meditation, WAiNK, challenges (10 trophies).
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & exploration. Card sets, vehicles, all 52 areas, garages, Scooter Bros registrations (5 trophies).
  5. Stage 5 — Combat & robberies. Wanted escapes, shop and vault robberies, Combat Focus, gangs (10 trophies).
  6. Stage 6 — Money & misc. Laundering, spending, the Ocean Beach cocktail, choices, and the strange event (6 trophies).
  7. Stage 7 — Cleanup. 100% completion, then the platinum (2 trophies).

Missable verdict — built from the official descriptions

No trophy in the official list is one-time-only. Nothing in the 55 descriptions says a trophy can only be earned at one point. The watch-list is three trophies tied to a story beat — #20 Baited My Tackle (Cal's fishing challenge), #45 Capital Offense (the showroom smash-out) and #47 Personal Best (a partnership moment). If one does not pop, replay the mission from the mission replay menu. Finish the stranger strands before the final mission, just in case the post-game state changes.
